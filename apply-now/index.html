Welcome to Women LinkUp / Exponenti’elles!

We are so pleased that you are interested in applying to our 12-month coaching program.

Women LinkUp/Exponenti’elles serves women and all gender-diverse individuals. We currently serve British Columbia, Alberta, Ontario, Quebec and Nova Scotia, and are committed to serving our clients in English or French anywhere within our service areas.

We want to make sure the program is a good experience and the right fit for you from the start and to ensure that you are in a place in your life where you have the space and time to make the most of it.

Our coaching program has some basic requirements, so please consider these before applying. Clients of our program are:

  • Currently experiencing low income or are in receipt of income/disability assistance (note that this is based on your personal income only).
  • Currently in a stable housing situation (note that shelters, transitional homes, addiction recovery centres, corrections-based facilities, living with family, friends, etc. are considered stable housing).
  • At a point of stability in health and safety to be able to commit to a 12-month intensive program and have other supports in place if needed.
  • Have Conversational English or French.
  • Women and gender-diverse individuals 18 years or older. We have no upper age limit.
  • Currently living in Canada (note that our program has no education or Canadian citizenship requirements).

If you have any questions about our program requirements or about the application process itself, please [email protected] and one of our staff members will be happy to assist you!
